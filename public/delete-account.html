Delete your Elizade Connect account

Elizade Connect is the customer app of Elizade Nigeria Limited. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time, with or without the app.

How to request deletion

Option 1: from the app

  1. Open Elizade Connect and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Privacy & Security.
  3. Tap Delete my account. This opens a pre-filled email to us.
  4. Send the email.

Option 2: without the app

  1. Email info@elizade.net from the email address you use to sign in to Elizade Connect.
  2. Use the subject line Account deletion request - Elizade Connect.
Email a deletion request

To protect your account, we will reply by email to confirm the request came from you before anything is deleted. Once confirmed, we complete the deletion within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number and profile photo
  • Your sign-in sessions and notification settings, including your device's notification token
  • Vehicles you added to My Vehicles, and their uploaded ownership documents
  • Photos you uploaded for trade-in valuations and warranty claims
  • Your support tickets, messages and attachments
  • Your saved vehicles, watchlist and availability alerts
  • Error reports from the app that are linked to your account

What we keep, and for how long

Some records must be kept even after your account is deleted, because the law requires it or because they are needed to honour a warranty or resolve a dispute:

  • Sales and payment records (quotations, reservations, invoices and receipts) are kept for 6 years to meet Nigerian accounting and tax requirements.
  • Service and warranty history stays linked to the vehicle's chassis number (VIN), not to your app account, for as long as the vehicle's warranty or recall obligations last.

These records are no longer linked to an app account and are deleted when the retention period ends.
